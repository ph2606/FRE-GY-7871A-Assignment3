# AI use disclosure

OpenAI Codex provided substantial assistance with reading the supplied
references, locating and downloading public sources, implementing the
acquisition and analysis code, building the notebook and report, and testing
the implementation. Parallel agents assisted with reference reviews, market
and social-source research, phrase and timing audits, methodology checks,
and documentation. Automated reviews are not independent student
verification or human ground-truth annotation.

The student supplied the assignment, course readings and submission
requirements, specified the active-conflict sample and requested coverage
of Truth Social while distinguishing platforms. This disclosure does not
claim additional student research or verification that is not documented.

The primary February 28–September 18, 2026 study uses an unsigned NLP
classification of relevant publisher coverage, with a retrospective strict
upper-quartile threshold. The downloaded snapshot yields 35 high-news and
105 low-news sessions and 34 main matched H/L pairs before outcome-specific
paired deletion. Market returns do not select the news regimes. The
implementation follows the paper's uncentered second moments, three
instrumental-variable estimators and conditional variance calculations.
Two of 19 available outcome rows have uncertain positive reference-variance
shifts; the other diagnostics do not establish a causal single-war-shock
model. GDELT event metadata supply a separate robustness comparison and
duration-news extension rather than the primary regime.

All empirical calculations use downloaded observations and stated
transformations. The data were frozen before September 21 trading, when
September 18 was the latest completed US cash-market session. Source lags
are explicit: exchange-traded inputs reach
September 18, CMT yields and credit OAS September 17, spot oil September 15,
and the fitted Treasury curve and broad dollar September 11. Missing
observations are not filled. January 1–February 27 material supplies context
outside the main financial sample. Raw responses and article/post datasets
remain local; no datasets are committed to the repository.

The phrase audit compares two explicit headline retrieval rules, preserves
source examples and distinguishes ceasefire extension, war extension,
negation, conditional language and operation names. Its additional
retrievals are candidates, not validated gains in accuracy. No independent
human-labeled Iran-war sentiment benchmark or inter-annotator assessment
was created. Synthetic examples appear only in software checks and are
identified as such.

The social appendix audits 404 Truth Social text candidates from January
1–September 18 using the retrieved CNN archive. It contains literal subject
and claim cues, attribution/repost checks, neutral qualitative interpretation
and scheduled cash-market timing. It does not claim a complete X/Twitter
archive, complete media coverage, verified authorship of every record,
calibrated war-end probabilities, numerical evaluations of a political
official or policy, or causal effects of individual posts. The proposed
intraday design is separate from the completed daily analysis.

No data, coefficients, empirical accuracy estimates or significance findings
were invented. The investment discussion uses a dated indicative Treasury
bill yield as a comparison asset; it is not an optimized backtest. No trade
was placed.
